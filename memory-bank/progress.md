## Known Issues

1. **Task Complexity Balance**:
   - Some tasks may be more complex than others
   - May need to adjust time expectations based on initial feedback

2. **Metrics Subjectivity**:
   - Self-assessed quality scores are inherently subjective
   - May need to normalize scores across participants

3. **Tool Familiarity Bias**:
   - Participants may be more familiar with Edge Copilot
   - Week 2 results may be affected by learning curve for GitHub Copilot

4. **RS Copilot Knowledge Variability**:
   - Varying levels of familiarity with RS Copilot patterns
   - May affect assessment of integration capabilities

5. **Analysis Limitations**:
   - Analysis scripts not yet tested with real data
   - May need adjustments once actual metrics are collected
